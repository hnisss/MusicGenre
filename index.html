MUSIC GENRES

JAZZ

Jazz is a music genre that originated in the United States, characterized by improvisation, swing rhythms, syncopation, and complex harmonies. It developed primarily within African American communities and draws strong influence from blues, ragtime, and spiritual music traditions.

EXPLORE

CLASSICAL

Classical music is rooted in Western art and culture, encompassing a vast history from the Medieval period to modern times. Known for its sophisticated compositions, orchestration, and structural rigor, classical music emphasizes rich acoustic instrumentation.

EXPLORE

POP

Pop music focuses on catchiness, melodic hooks, and accessible song structures. Evolving continuously with production trends, it blends elements of dance, electronic, and vocal harmonies to produce chart-topping anthems.

EXPLORE

HIP HOP

Hip hop emerged as a cultural movement driven by rhythmic beats, rhyming vocals (rapping), DJing, and sampling. Characterized by poetic storytelling and bass-heavy production, it reflects personal, social, and political expressions.

EXPLORE

ROCK

Rock music centered around the electric guitar, bass, and drums. Emerging in the 1950s, it spans subgenres from energetic indie and alternative rock to heavy metal, defined by expressive riffs and driving rhythms.

EXPLORE

INDIE

Indie music represents independent, DIY music creation characterized by authentic storytelling, unique instrumental textures, and atmospheric soundscapes, free from traditional commercial constraints.

EXPLORE

EDM

Electronic Dance Music (EDM) is crafted primarily for nightclubs, festivals, and raves. Built on synthesized melodies, rhythmic drops, and energetic basslines, EDM drives modern electronic production worldwide.

EXPLORE

R&B

Rhythm and Blues (R&B) combines soulful vocal arrangements, smooth melodies, and groove-driven beats. Evolving from blues and gospel roots, modern R&B incorporates pop, electronic, and hip-hop influences.
